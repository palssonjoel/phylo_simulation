## 17-09-2026
Two issues with the simulation: 1) many die out, and 2) export-heavy countries
such as Denmark have low cumulative cases owing to a low probability of import.
Instead of adjusting the transition matrix to include a within-state move probability
(i.e. increasing diagonal from 0), I think a better approach is to adjust pMove.

pMove describes the probability for a host at any timepoint to undergo a transition,
and the transition matrix then defines to where this transition occurs. Right now,
pMove is a constant 0.1, and lowering this would lead to more within-country evolution.

However, pMove should perhaps mirror trade rates. A pig in Denmark, which exports a lot
should arguably have a higher probability of moving to another country than say a
pig in Germany. This argues in favor of different pMove for different countries,
which could be adjusted to export/import rates. 

I tested reducing it to 0.01, and this increased cumulative cases in Denmark, 
and reduced them in the high-import countries such as Hungary, Sweden, and Germany.

## 03-10-2026
Density dependence is the way to go, I think. I've created two different versions: (1) a suppression factor
is activated at a global threshold of active hosts, e.g. 1000 or 10,000. This effectively tapers off the spread
and slows it to an endemic pattern, while still allowing high export countries to maintain a population of hosts.
Or (2), in which the suppression factor kicks in at a given percentage of the total pig population in each country. Testing it now, I set it to 1%. This means that when the number of active, infective hosts are 1% of the total state population, p_trans will be multiplied by < 0.5. As it keeps growing, this factor will grow. This is the one I'm testing on the HPC now, with time ranges of 1, 5, 10, and 20 years. The hope is that spread can be maintained for at least 5 years without dying out in Denmark or the growth being explosive.

However, 1% still allows for active host numbers in the tens of thousands in e.g. Germany and Denmark. As such, it might not give the strength of suppression I want. Thus, I will later also try to set up the global threshold method, and set it at e.g. 5,000
