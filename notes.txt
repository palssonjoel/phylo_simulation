## 17-09-2026
Two issues with the simulation: 1) many die out, and 2) export-heavy countries
such as Denmark have low cumulative cases owing to a low probability of import.
Instead of adjusting the transition matrix to include a within-state move probability
(i.e. increasing diagonal from 0), I think a better approach is to adjust pMove.

pMove describes the probability for a host at any timepoint to undergo a transition,
and the transition matrix then defines to where this transition occurs. Right now,
pMove is a constant 0.1, and lowering this would lead to more within-country evolution.

However, pMove should perhaps mirror trade rates. A pig in Denmark, which exports a lot
should arguably have a higher probability of moving to another country than say a
pig in Germany. This argues in favor of different pMove for different countries,
which could be adjusted to export/import rates. 

I tested reducing it to 0.01, and this increased cumulative cases in Denmark, 
and reduced them in the high-import countries such as Hungary, Sweden, and Germany.
